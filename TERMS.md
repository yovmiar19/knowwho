# KnowWho — Terms of Service

Last updated: 21 November 2026

KnowWho is a Forge app built for the Atlassian Codegeist 2026 hackathon. It recommends experts for Jira issues using the Teamwork Graph, Confluence authorship, past fixes, component ownership, and current workload.

## Use

- The app is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind.
- The app reads Jira, Confluence, and Teamwork Graph data on your Atlassian site to generate rankings. It does not store personal data.
- You are responsible for ensuring you have the right to install the app on your site and for approving the scopes it requests (read access to Jira, Confluence, and Teamwork Graph data).

# KnowWho — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

KnowWho is a Forge app that helps Jira teams find the right person to review, own, or unblock an issue.

## What data the app accesses

When you use KnowWho, the app reads the following data from your Atlassian site, in real time, solely to generate an expert ranking for the Jira issue you are viewing:

- Jira issue details (summary, description, components, assignee, comments, and resolution history)
- Confluence pages related to the issue topic, and their authors
- Teamwork Graph data (authorship, past work, team membership, and assignments)

## What data the app stores

**KnowWho does not collect, store, or share personal data.**

- The app does not copy or store personal data (including Atlassian account IDs) in any system outside Atlassian.
- The app does not cache data for longer than 24 hours. In fact, rankings are computed on demand and no user data is persisted at all.
- All data stays within the Atlassian platform; the app runs on Atlassian Forge and makes no calls to third-party servers.
